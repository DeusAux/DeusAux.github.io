Features

Everything a virtual pianist needs

Built for QWERTY-based pianos like the ones on Roblox and online piano sites.

Autoplay

Paste a sheet and GN plays it for you, with live tempo, transpose, and humanize controls so it never sounds robotic.

Synthesia Reader

Point it at a falling-note piano video on screen and it reads the notes into a playable sheet — no manual transcribing.

Record & Playback

Record your own key presses, including how long each note is held, then play them back note-for-note.

MIDI Converter

Drop in a MIDI file and turn it into a QWERTY sheet or a ready-to-play recording.

Sheet Library

Search community sheet boards and keep your own favourites, all inside the app.

Your layout, your look

Remap keys for 88-key, 61-key, or custom layouts, and pick from six built-in colour themes.

Questions

Good to know

What do I need to run it?

A Windows 10 or newer PC. GN Pianist runs alongside any browser-based or desktop virtual piano.

How many devices can I use?

Each license activates on one device at a time. If you need to move it to a new PC, email us and we'll reset it for you.

What's your refund policy?

Because license keys are delivered instantly, purchases are final. The exception is a genuine access or compatibility problem — if the app won't work for you, contact us and we'll make it right or refund you.

Do I need to log in every time?

No. You enter your key once on first launch. After that the app opens straight away and quietly keeps your license valid in the background.
